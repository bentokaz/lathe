LATHE

Make music in your browser: write, record, mix and score to video.

Best on a bigger screen

Lathe is a full music studio with a timeline, an editor and a mixer, and it needs a laptop, desktop or Chromebook screen to fit. On a phone it is cramped, and some parts will not fit.

About Lathe

Lathe is a music studio that runs in the browser: tracks, sampled and synthesised instruments, a piano roll, styles that guide you through a genre, a mixer with effects and automation, video scoring, and export to audio, MIDI and video. Made by bentokaz as a school project. The code is on GitHub.

Everything else
Guitars, ukulele and other plucked strings, the synths and the drum kits are generated by Lathe as you play; no recordings.
